# Inspecting a render

Listen to the rendered WAV directly whenever audio inspection is available, and inspect both panels of the feedback image (`_spec.png`) alongside the diagnostics JSON. The waveform panel displays instantaneous peak amplitude, stereo L/R balance, and peak time; the spectrogram panel displays log-frequency energy relative to that file's peak (`-80 dB` to `0 dB`). Use `scripts/cli.py compare a.wav b.wav` to verify metric shifts across passes.

| What you observe | What to change |
| --- | --- |
| Hollow or buzzy low thumps that dominate each event | Check `band_energy_pct["below_180_hz"]` (`energy_below_180_hz_pct`). Reduce the lowest modes, raise the noise band cutoff, or replace loud pitch-gliding sines on rigid slams (which sound like a hollow tom or wooden dowel) with broadband `35–190 Hz` sub-thud, double-staged impacts, and damped `resonate` body modes. |
| Creak sounds like a zipper, voice, or bottle whistle | `20–130 Hz` impulse trains read like a zipper/ratchet, sweeping `500–1200 Hz` periodic pulses across multi-formant `modes` reads vocal/creature-like, and narrowband noise-fed bandpass filters sound like whistling into a bottle. Reserve `stick_slip` for geared tracks, ratchets, and slow dry catches (`15–80 Hz`), and avoid simulating bowed/rusty metal squeaks with filtered noise or simple pulse trains. |
| Machinery or sci-fi SFX sounds like an Atari game | Remove naked `tonal` sine sweeps and dry arcade beeps from foreground mechanisms. Build realism from crisp relay snaps, resonant metal scrapes (`friction` + `resonate`), multi-layered turbulent air ducts, heavy geared `stick_slip` clatter, and broadband impacts. |
| Repeated broadband columns with nearly identical shape | Vary contact-to-release timing and pressure knots, especially secondary bursts. Give each contact fresh RNG draws and varied band limits. |
| A voice sounds like an oscillator or every cry repeats | Shape each call's pitch, `pressure`, `opening`, and `strain` independently. Let formants sweep across `opening`, confine `strain` to stressed segments, and taper pressure into breath. |
| A ringing object resembles a static chord | Let brighter modes decay faster and keep the longest decay on one or two low modes. Split a mode slightly for slow beating and separate strike and ring timbres. |
| A crowd or ambience sounds centered or static | Build independent layers with `canvas.new_layer()`, place them across `pan` and `distance` in `StereoScene`, and add shared space with `apply_room`. Keep foreground peaks controlled so background detail remains audible. |
| A smooth rectangular cloud sounds like static | Shape load and release with `pressure` knots, combine grains of differing sizes, leave brief motion gaps, and sweep color with `friction(..., end_band=...)`. |
| Unnatural harshness or fizz above 7 kHz | Check `band_energy_pct["above_7000_hz"]`. Shorten bright contacts, narrow the high band, apply `distance_filter` / `distance` in `StereoScene`, or increase room `damping`. |
| Soft transients or ringing before contact | Place crisp attacks in waveform primitives (`contact`, `granular`) and lower `spectral_gain` if Griffin–Lim paint smears leading edges. |
| Peak hits `-1 dB` but the body feels thin or quiet | Check `crest_factor_db` (`peak_db - active_rms_db`) and `peak_time_sec` in the waveform panel. Values above ~18–22 dB on continuous foley often point to one isolated transient spike; tame the outlier or use `soft_limit(ceiling_db=-1.0, drive=...)` so the body fills out without clipping. |
| Tails stop abruptly or clip at canvas end | Check `tail_rms_db` (RMS of the final 20 ms) and `active_end_sec`. Extend canvas duration, pull events earlier, let modes decay cleanly, or call `scene.fade_edges(fade_out=...)` so `tail_rms_db` settles well below `active_rms_db` (ideally `< -60 dB` for finite events). |
| Non-zero baseline shift in waveform panel | Check `dc_offset`; keep near `0.0` (e.g. `< 1e-3`). Highpass or avoid asymmetric waveforms if offset drifts. |

`weighted_centroid_hz` weights stronger frames more heavily and tracks brightness shifts across passes. `active_rms_db` excludes 10 ms blocks more than 40 dB below the peak block. Compare passes with `target_peak_db=None` (or `cli.py compare`) so peak normalization does not disguise real level changes. For stereo clips, verify `mono_rms_db` is close to `rms_db` and avoid sustained negative `stereo_correlation`.
